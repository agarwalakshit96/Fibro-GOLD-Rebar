The idea

Strength, without corrosion.

The mark is an F built from three rebar segments. The diagonal ribs are the helical surface of a GFRP bar, cut out of the gold so the mark works in one colour, on any background. Charcoal carries engineering authority; one restrained gold carries the premium. Most competitors sit in generic blue and green, so charcoal and gold is open ground.

Charcoal

Engineering weight, trust, readable in the field and in print.

Gold

The premium cue. Used sparingly, never as a gradient or metallic effect.

Ribbed F

Reads as rebar at a glance, and as the initial of Fibro.

Space & size

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space

Keep free space around the logo equal to the width of the F's stem (about 1/6 of the mark height) on every side. Nothing else sits inside it.

Minimum size

  • Horizontal lock-up: 32 mm wide in print, 140 px on screen.
  • Stacked lock-up: 20 mm wide in print, 96 px on screen.
  • Mark alone: 8 mm / 24 px. Below 32 px use the solid favicon (no ribs).
  • Under the minimum, drop the "By Fibrotech FRP" line before shrinking the logo, and use the mark alone.

Voice

Plain, precise, proven

We say

  • "Does not corrode."
  • "Datasheet and test report on request."
  • "Ask our design team before you specify."
  • "Lap length and cover: see the design guide."

We avoid

  • "World-class", "revolutionary", "unbeatable".
  • "India's largest / first / best."
  • "100-year life" or any lifespan without data.
  • "Stronger than steel" without saying in which property.

Claims

Certification wording, by stage

Every asset carries a swappable certification block so the wording changes in one place when a stage is reached.

Now

Corrosion-free GFRP rebar. Specification and datasheet on request. No BIS, ISI, NHAI or standards-compliance wording.

With independent test reports

"Tested to IS 18255:2023", report available to download.

BIS application filed

"BIS licence applied (ref. ____)". Only with the reference on record.

BIS licence granted

"BIS licensed – CM/L-_______" with the number, scope and plant exactly as on the licence.

Rules

Keep the mark intact

Do

  • Use supplied SVG/PNG files; never redraw.
  • Use the dark version on charcoal, the light version on light surfaces.
  • Keep the By Fibrotech FRP line on first-contact material.
  • Place on plain areas of photos, or use the mono white version.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or add gradients or metallic effects.
  • Recolour the mark or wordmark outside the supplied sets.
  • Put the gold version on a mid-tone or busy background.
  • Separate "Fibro" and "Gold", or set the name in another font.
