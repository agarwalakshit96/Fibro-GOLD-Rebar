# Fibro Gold – Brand Foundation (v1, pre-licence launch)

**Brand:** Fibro Gold  **Endorsed by:** Fibrotech FRP  **Product:** straight GFRP (glass-fibre reinforced polymer) rebar
**Markets:** infrastructure contractors (NHAI/PWD/metro), builders/architects/consultants, dealers/distributors, export (Gulf, Africa)

## 1. Essence
**Strength, without corrosion.**
Fibro Gold exists because steel rusts and concrete pays for it. We make reinforcement that does not corrode, and we back it with the data and design support specifiers need.

## 2. Positioning statement
For contractors, consultants, builders and distributors who need reinforcement that will not corrode, Fibro Gold is the GFRP rebar brand from Fibrotech FRP that pairs engineered composite bars with open technical documentation and specifier support, so teams can specify and buy with confidence.

## 3. Where we compete (from research)
Competitors mostly sell on "India's largest" and "BIS" claims, publish little technical data, and show prices only on request. Fibro Gold wins on **clarity and proof**: plain specs, downloadable data, design guidance, and honest claims. We cannot use certification claims at launch (see section 7), so documentation and support carry the brand until they are earned.

## 4. Brand pillars
1. **Corrosion-free by nature.** GFRP does not rust. Lead with this category fact.
2. **Documented, not just described.** Every claim has a data sheet, test report or standard behind it, shown as soon as it exists.
3. **Specifier-first support.** Design guidance, comparison tables, a calculator and fast technical answers.
4. **Dependable supply.** Consistent bars, traceable lots, clear lead times (activate when the plant is live).
5. **Backed by Fibrotech FRP.** An established FRP company behind a new brand.

## 5. Voice
Precise, calm, confident, plain English. Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives. Engineers should trust it and a site supervisor should understand it.
- Do: "Does not corrode." "Tested to IS 18255:2023 — report attached." (only once true)
- Don't: "World-class", "revolutionary", "the best", "unbeatable".
- Gold is a colour and a standard of finish, not a boast. Never use "gold standard" as copy.

## 6. Tagline options
| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Strength, without corrosion.** (recommended) | Literal, ownable, claim-safe, translates well |
| Reinforce for the long term. | Softer; avoid years claims |
| Engineered to outlast rust. | Memorable; needs durability data to back "outlast" |
| Rebar that never rusts. | Punchy; GFRP does not rust, but keep to this wording only |
Descriptor line (always paired with the logo in print): **GFRP Rebar**.

## 7. Claims ladder (update as certifications arrive)
| Stage | May say | May not say |
|---|---|---|
| Launch (now) | GFRP does not corrode; lightweight (cite own data once measured); made by Fibrotech FRP; specification and data sheet "on request" | "BIS certified/licensed", ISI mark, "IS 18256 compliant", "NHAI/IRC approved", "India's largest/first/best", any lifespan in years |
| Independent test reports in hand (NABL or equivalent) | "Tested to IS 18255:2023" with the report downloadable | "BIS certified" |
| BIS application filed | "BIS licence applied (ref. ____)" | "BIS certified" |
| BIS licence granted | "BIS licensed – CM/L-_______" with number, scope and plant as on the licence | Anything outside the licence scope |
Rules: no claim without a document we can send on request. All numbers come from our own test reports, not competitor or vendor-blog figures. Verify IS 18255/18256 limits against the purchased standards before quoting any.

## 8. Messaging by audience
- **Infrastructure contractors:** Fewer repairs from rebar corrosion; lighter bars to handle; documented material for approvals.
- **Consultants/architects:** Clear specs, design guidance, referenced standards, fast technical response.
- **Builders:** Straightforward supply, sizes, lead times, site handling tips.
- **Dealers/distributors:** A clear brand, a growing technical library, support materials, honest margins and territory terms (to be defined).
- **Export (Gulf/Africa):** Test documentation packs and standards references (ASTM D7957, ACI 440 family) once available; origin and quality documentation.

## 9. Naming and lock-up rules
- Write the brand as **Fibro Gold** (two words, title case). Logo uses capitals: FIBRO GOLD.
- Endorsement line **By Fibrotech FRP** appears with the logo on first contact material (website header, brochure cover, packaging, invoices). Social avatars may use the mark alone.
- Fibrotech FRP is the company; Fibro Gold is the product brand. Contracts, GST and legal text use the company name.

## 10. Boilerplate
**Short:** Fibro Gold is a GFRP rebar brand from Fibrotech FRP, making corrosion-free reinforcement for infrastructure and construction.
**Long:** Fibro Gold, from Fibrotech FRP, supplies straight GFRP rebar for roads, bridges, marine and coastal works, water and wastewater structures, and industrial construction. Because glass-fibre reinforced polymer does not corrode, it removes a main cause of concrete deterioration. Fibro Gold backs the product with technical documentation and design support for contractors, consultants, builders and distributors.
